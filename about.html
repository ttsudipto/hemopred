HemoPred - Prediction of HbA2 and HbF from CBCs and RBC indices

Home About Help Team
  • HemoPred is a web-based tool to predict hemoglobin A2 (HbA2) and fetal hemoglobin (HbF) from routine blood test features
  • Regression models with Mixture of Experts (MoE) approach were developed to perform the predictions.
  • The features used are as follows:
    • Age
    • Hemoglobin (Hb)
    • Hematocrit (HCT)
    • Red Blood Ćell (RBC) count
    • Mean Corpuscular Volume (MCV)
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin (MCH)
    • Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration (MCHC)
    • Red cell Distribution Width - Coefficient of Variation (RDW-CV)
    • White Blood Cell (WBC) count
    • Platelet count
  • The models were trained on an Indian dataset of 5314 individuals.

    Disease/Diagnostic label Number of patients

  • A subset of 80% data was used to develop the models with cross validation, and remaining 20% was used for held-out internal validation. Performance of the MoE models is given below.

    Task 5-fold cross validation Held-out internal validation
    Mean absolute error R2-score Mean absolute error R2-score
    HbA2 prediction 0.1654 0.8442 0.1550 0.8605
    HbF prediction 0.1394 0.6055 0.1436 0.6062

  • SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) was used to compute feature contributions
